How to play

Trust the conversation, then doubt it.

You get a short chat with an unknown opponent. Ask, observe, and decide whether the subject is human or AI.

01

Start simple

Open with casual questions. Humans usually reveal rhythm, context, and small inconsistencies without trying.

02

Apply pressure

Ask a follow-up. Switch topics. Look for replies that feel too polished, evasive, or strangely consistent.

03

Cast the verdict

When time runs out, choose Human or AI. The game reveals the truth after your verdict.
